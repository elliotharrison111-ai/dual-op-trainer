Settings

Gimbal (camera op)

Gimbal profile in use C cycles them

The RC Plus tops out at 270°/s pan and 120°/s tilt. Controllable tilt is −90° to +30° and pan stops at 300° either side of the nose.

Aircraft (pilot)

Flight mode in use F cycles T, N, S

Capped at the Inspire 3's published limits: 26 m/s, 8 m/s climb and descent, 150°/s yaw. Responsiveness is how hard it accelerates and brakes.
