Settings

Gimbal (camera op)

Gimbal profile in use C cycles them

Published Inspire 3 figures: tilt −90° to +30° without the extension; pan stops at 300° either side of the nose. DJI doesn't publish what the smoothness and EXP settings do mathematically, so the response curve here is an estimate.

Aircraft (pilot)

Flight mode in use F cycles T, N, S

Capped at the Inspire 3's published limits: 26 m/s, 8 m/s climb and descent, 150°/s yaw. Responsiveness is how hard it accelerates and brakes. Higher EXP softens small stick inputs around centre; roll has its own. In A mode the sticks set the lean angle (up to 35°), so max speed and responsiveness don't apply.

Wind

Gusts

The Inspire 3 is rated for 14 m/s (31 mph) in flight and 12 m/s for takeoff and landing. In T, N and S it leans into the wind and holds its spot; gusts knock it off briefly. In A mode nothing holds it, so it drifts with the wind. Wind is lighter near the ground.

Physics

v2 steps the physics at 240 Hz, makes the aircraft tilt before it accelerates so moves start smoothly, limits yaw and climb jerk, thins the air with altitude, and has the AI pilot fly the same model through virtual sticks, so it feels gusts and obeys the mode limits. Best scores are kept separately for v1 and v2.
