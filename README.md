# Dual-Op Trainer

A browser simulator for practising dual-operator car shots on a DJI Inspire 3: a camera operator on the gimbal, a pilot on the aircraft, or both at once with two controllers. Shots are scored on framing, smoothness, holding the line and keeping clear of the car.

**Open it:** https://elliotharrison111-ai.github.io/dual-op-trainer/ in Chrome, Edge or Brave on a computer.

## Controllers

- **DJI RC 2 or RC Plus:** power the aircraft off, plug the controller in with a data USB-C cable, then Controls → **Connect DJI controller (USB)** → pick it → **Stick setup**.
- **DJI RC-N1, RC-N2, RC-N3:** Controls → **Connect RC-N serial**.
- **Gamepads and RC transmitters in USB joystick mode:** plug in and press a button.
- Keyboard and mouse work without a controller.

Connect a second controller for the Dual seat and assign each one under Seats.

## Locations

The practice valley is built in, and so is **Big Sky, Montana** (Lone Mountain Trail). Location → **Make a map** builds other US locations from public map data.

Map data: elevation from USGS 3DEP, aerial imagery from the USGS National Map, roads from US Census TIGER/Line.

Scores, takes and recorded shots stay in each person's browser. Not affiliated with DJI. Flight and gimbal behaviour is a training approximation.
